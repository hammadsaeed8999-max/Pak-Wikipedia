About PAK-ARMY

The Pakistan Army is the land-based branch of the Pakistan Armed Forces and plays an important role in protecting the country’s sovereignty, territorial integrity, and national security. It was established after the independence of Pakistan in 1947, when the military forces of British India were divided between Pakistan and India. Since then, the Pakistan Army has developed into a large and organized institution with personnel serving across different regions of the country. Its responsibilities include defending Pakistan’s borders, supporting national security operations, and assisting the government during emergencies and natural disasters. Throughout Pakistan’s history, army personnel have also participated in United Nations peacekeeping missions in different parts of the world.


Organization, Training and Capabilities

The Pakistan Army consists of different branches and specialized units that work together to perform military and security responsibilities. These include infantry, armoured corps, artillery, engineers, aviation, air defence, signals, and special operations units. Training focuses on discipline, physical fitness, leadership, teamwork, military skills, and professional development. The Army also operates training institutions and academies where officers and soldiers receive education and specialized instruction. Modern communications, vehicles, aircraft, equipment, and other technologies are used to support military operations. The Pakistan Military Academy (PMA) at Kakul is one of the country’s major institutions for officer training.


Service to Pakistan

In addition to its defence responsibilities, the Pakistan Army has frequently been involved in humanitarian assistance and disaster-relief activities. During floods, earthquakes, landslides, and other emergencies, army personnel have helped with rescue operations, transportation, medical assistance, and relief distribution. Army medical facilities and welfare organizations also provide services in various parts of the country. Pakistani soldiers have served in international peacekeeping operations under the United Nations, contributing to missions aimed at maintaining peace and supporting affected communities. The Pakistan Army therefore represents an important institution within Pakistan’s national defence system, with its activities extending beyond military duties to include emergency assistance and international peacekeeping.
